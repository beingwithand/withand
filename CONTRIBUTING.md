\# Engineering \& Governance Standards



\## The "72-Hour Rule"

All Pull Requests affecting `/src` or `/amendments` are automatically placed in a `status:cooling-off` state for 72 hours. Do not ping maintainers for faster merges.



\## Commit Message Convention

We follow \[Conventional Commits](https://www.conventionalcommits.org/):

\* `feat:` (New logic)

\* `fix:` (Bug fix)

\* `gov:` (Constitutional amendment)

\* `doc:` (Documentation only)



\*\*Example:\*\* `gov: amend heuristic H-001 to reduce damping weight`
